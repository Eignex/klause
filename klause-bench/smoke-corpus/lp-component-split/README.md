# Independent LP smoke models

The isolated-cover MPS pair has twelve independent 32-column integer cycles. Each
edge constrains the sum of its two coordinates between 96–102 and 145–151.
Setting every cycle coordinate to 60 satisfies these rows.

A separate three-column block demands at least three in every pairwise sum.
The conflict source caps the total at four. Summing its three lower rows forces
the total to be at least 4.5. The feasible source caps the total at five and
admits (2, 2, 1) on that block.
