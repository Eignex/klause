# 15.2 Preparation and definitions

Before the walk of [Strategies and moves](15.1-strategies.md) can start, a run
needs an invariant projection of its model and an initial assignment. Many models
also contain coordinates that are functions of others: a product's result, an
Element's selected value, a channelled indicator. Searching those coordinates
independently would waste moves and break relations the source states exactly, so
local search maintains them as one-way definitions and backsolves repairs through
them. This subchapter follows preparation, initialization, the materialization of
source definitions, and the repairs and channels built on them.

## Projections and preparation

The immutable local-search projection is prepared on demand and reused across
draws. Mixed-pool arms over the same model share it, including ALNS's inner local
search; objective-bound overlays keep their own. Each Boolean, integer and real
variable's occurrence index lists each active factor once, in factor order, so a
move updates every invariant reading the coordinate exactly once, including
positions with different presence literals. Assignments, random generators,
weights and invariant payloads belong to each live state. Immutable per-factor
lookups are shared with the projection and allocate no mutable per-worker cache.

Invariants substitute a new value into every position that reads the moved
coordinate. Product scoring and repeated operands of fixed-power chains follow this
rule; product residuals subtract the result exactly before capping the distance, and
overflow uses a private 128-bit temporary, so an overflowing product cannot satisfy
a signed-word endpoint. Increasing chains add their strict gap only after
subtraction and decline unrepresentable successors.

Preparation is resumable. A handle retains private cursors for state buffers,
assignment loading or randomization, implicit seed election, definition readers,
Boolean repair graphs, warm weights, greedy coordinate order and factor payloads.
Cancellation is polled before each retained step, and explicit loops advance in
batches of at most 256 coordinates, words, factors or reader edges. Random Boolean
words precede integer draws, followed by anchored integer draws, pins and real
starts; a suspension draws no extra random values and repeats no completed work.
Partial assignments, costs and warm captures stay private until all initial factors
are scored and the initial greedy pass completes. Shared preparation steps are
published under a mutex only as complete indexes, and a suspended arm holds no lock.
Preparation consumes time but no search instructions or move allowance.

These boundaries are cooperative, not hard elapsed-time bounds. Each primitive
buffer allocation, array copy and collection resize remains atomic, as do one
factor visit, definition evaluation, factor initialization or implicit global seed.
[Portfolio preparation revisits](16.2-portfolio-scheduling.md#preparation-revisits) admit every
initial sibling before extra visits.

## Initialization and implicit table moves

`GreedyInit` walks the variables in shuffled order and commits, for each, the value
that minimizes the current cost: a Boolean flip is kept unless it raises the cost,
and an integer variable tries every value of a domain of at most 16 values or 16
sampled ones. It is one forward pass that never raises the cost; the aim is a
low-violation start, not feasibility. During that repair, payloads, costs and
definitions update normally while neighbour configuration marking and tabu tracking
are suppressed. Resumable optimization retains the shuffled order and next
coordinate across slices and completes the initial or post-restart repair before
entering the walk. Coordinate eligibility uses the move sink's pinned, defined and
implicit-owner filters, preserving seeded globals and one-way definitions.

Implicit seeding gives elected structural globals a satisfying start. Implicit table
moves select values from a support row intersected with root domains; interval and
wildcard cells remain searchable, and repeated variables use the intersection of
their cells. Frozen coordinates keep their values, an unreachable row produces no
partial move or seed, and tables reading defined integers decline implicit seeding
because a definition could rewrite them outside the chosen row. Built-in random
restarts apply implicit seeding and the definition sweep before initializing factor
costs; custom restart policies keep their ordinary protocol.

## Source definition materialization

Definitions come from two places. A source can supply neutral `DefinitionHint`
relations, as FlatZinc does through `SourceSetup`, and the selected consumer can
infer native definitions from its working factors. `DefinitionalSweep.fromSource`
reads the hints from an unchanged source problem and builds the dependency graph
with separate integer and Boolean namespaces. Without hints, the consumer still
maintains unhinted absolute-value, product, integer-power, quotient and remainder
outputs.

The view is built only on demand. Loading builds no sweep; `PortfolioBuilder`
requests the native view after composing the actual local-search or ALNS
configurations and checking their source admission, and a complete-only pool never
requests it. Immutable descriptors can be shared; each worker constructs its own
invariant network and objective state. Original coordinates are mapped through the
retained source plan and the actual presolve mapping, excluding coordinates
recovered by reconstruction; opaque reconstruction withholds hint projection.
Construction spends the containing allowance, and cancellation discards the whole
view. A declined view keeps ordinary heuristic execution and every source
constraint.

Competing outputs remain searched, and iterative ordering leaves cyclic outputs and
cones that depend on them searched. Source bounds decide when signed-word node
kernels are safe; wider intermediates use exact integer evaluation, and integer
outputs that can equal the reserved no-write sentinel stay searched. Strict Boolean
comparisons keep exact endpoints. Compatibility Boolean folds join the move view
when they are unit folds or when all their inputs carry objective weight; other
folds remain searched for feasibility repair.

`functionalObjective(LinearObjective)` builds an optional gradient view from the
materialized cones. It agrees with the canonical objective at every feasible
assignment and leaves that objective unchanged. Source factors remain enforced, and
hints grant no feasibility or proof authority.

## Definitions and repairs

A maintained definition is a one-way node: when its inputs move, the network
recomputes the output in topological order. Defined outputs are not searched, and
independent constraints on a defined output still contribute violations. Requests
to change a maintained output become **inverse repairs** that change admissible
searched inputs; every target must lie in its root domain and pass pin, frozen and
implicit-owner filters, and the complete move is graded before acceptance.

The node families share that contract:

- **Absolute value.** Repairs propose the output magnitude or either admissible
  operand sign, following at most sixteen nested definitions; signed-word overflow
  stays violated.
- **Integer power.** Exact signed-word evaluation precedes domain clipping. Repairs
  offer the current power and every admissible exact signed root of the requested
  output, through at most sixteen nested powers.
- **Minimum and maximum.** Hinted extrema, their affine operands and aliases form a
  cone. Repairs backsolve affine rows and extrema into searched inputs; non-witness
  operands receive the required bound.
- **Unit Boolean folds** backsolve their signed source through nested folds.

Shared-input repairs recompute every affected definition before checking the
complete maintained cone. Functional objectives seed both integer and Boolean
leaves, and Boolean objective-bound moves use the fold inverse before ordinary
scoring.

Each state owns its traversal workspace for the current network. It marks each
affected node once, visits readers and sorts the live prefix into topological
order, retaining no assignment-dependent cone between moves. Coordinate saving
records each touched coordinate once and restores it through the incremental
primitives. Reentrant move application, probing or network replacement on the same
state throws before mutating scratch.

Boolean-only clause models with maintained unit definitions close native proposals
under clause implications before ranking. Each state builds the implication graph
under its exact pins during initialization. A proposal follows forced literals; at
a feasible point mandatory implications reach a fixpoint before a newly broken
longer clause receives a replacement supporter, ranked by break score, objective
delta and literal order. Conflicts and cancellation reject the whole proposal, and
only changed decision coordinates enter the move. These closures are heuristic
proposals, not deductions: every clause, price and pin is retained for full
grading and publication checks.

### Integer division outputs

A retained `DivMod` supplies exact graded checks and simultaneous-role move deltas.
Unique acyclic quotient and remainder outputs follow dividend and divisor moves
through the network; a zero divisor leaves the outputs untouched and the factor
violated. Direct repairs offer quotient and remainder updates together, reconstruct
a dividend from the current pair, and give an exact divisor repair when the
dividend minus the remainder is divisible by a nonzero quotient.
[Source semantics](03.1-source-relations.md#integer-division-relations) fix the rounding
convention.

### Element results

Hinted Element results are maintained from their index and selected cell; writes to
unselected cells skip the result cone. Value-directed repairs change an admissible
selected cell or backsolve a matching index through its source coordinates, scanning
at most 32 array positions, offering at most four alternatives and following at most
16 nested selections. A violation repair keeps its first reachable matching index and
may try two more from a rotating scan whose progress belongs to the state's payload.
Index arithmetic uses Long values, including offsets that cross the Int range. An
out-of-range index or a clamped result leaves the defining constraint to ordinary
violation scoring.

### Affine inverse repairs

Element index repairs backsolve affine definitions with one or two integer inputs
and a unit output coefficient, and hinted affine chains feeding only unary equality
predicates use the same inverse. Inverse repairs follow at most 16 aliases, checking
each intermediate domain and rejecting conflicting targets for a shared leaf.
Single-coordinate repairs precede joint moves, which enumerate at most 64 values of
one input domain. Affine aliases over extrema use the extremum inverse. Overflow,
nonintegral inverses and unsupported definitions decline the candidate without
stopping the remaining scan.

## Maintained literal channels

A literal channel links a Boolean indicator to a unary predicate over an integer,
such as `b ⇔ x = 3`. Inference derives a channel only from a unique unit unary
equality, disequality or ordered comparison over 0/1 bounds whose other Boolean
occurrences are channels; negative coefficients reverse ordered comparisons. Mixed
integer and Boolean definitions are ordered together, and competing or cyclic
outputs stay searched. Diversification kicks never flip maintained Boolean outputs
directly.

Repairs into a channel change an admissible Boolean input or backsolve the predicate
into a searched integer. Ordered inverses round toward the requested truth through
domain holes and reject unreachable thresholds; false equality targets sample at most
four alternatives from at most 32 present values. Literal chains stop at depth 16.
Clause, cardinality and weighted Boolean-row repairs backsolve equality predicates
through their integer inputs after binary lowering removes the integer channels.
Pinned values survive evaluation, and implicitly owned outputs are not rewritten
during per-move propagation; their definition factors keep any resulting violation.

Functional objective inference follows admitted predicates, channels, folds and
arithmetic definitions across both coordinate spaces in their shared topological
order. Domain-clipped, pinned or owned outputs keep ordinary constraint violations
and establish no source feasibility. Boolean break and make vectors initialize on
their first score query and are maintained incrementally afterwards.

## Coordinated channels and tuple moves

Value-driven moves carry equality indicator flips through directly connected unit
equality channels over 0/1 integers, so a single `IntSet` moves its indicators with
it. Targets must be present in the root domain and pass the generic filters; a
claimed coordinate is never overwritten, and repeated Boolean flips are read by
their final parity. Product definitions then maintain their outputs from the
coordinated changes.

Violated Table rows keep single-coordinate repairs and add at most two joint tuple
repairs. Joint repairs and structured tuple jumps carry indicators and channels with
every table coordinate protected from counter-shifts; a protected changed coordinate
rejects the whole tuple move, preserving the chosen row's support. A maintained unary
affine definition with zero offset and positive scale can invert zero or scale
targets through a maintained binary channel. Satisfied linear equalities
counter-shift the first admissible partner, in coefficient-magnitude order, whose
coefficient divides the driving drift.
