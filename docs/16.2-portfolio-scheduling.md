# 16.2 Scheduling

With publication rules fixed, the scheduler decides which retained worker runs next
and for how long. It hands out bounded slices of one shared schedule, lets expensive
preparation proceed across slices, guarantees complete search a share of time before
the first witness, and reseeds workers whose progress has stalled. None of these
decisions carries witness or proof authority; they only order work under the
allowances of [chapter 11](11-resources.md).

## Slice accounting and coverage

The counted schedule starts at 5000 node equivalents, grows by 1.5 after an ordinary
scheduled segment, and caps at 150000. Growth belongs to the shared schedule, rather
than to each arm. Initial probes and private-preparation revisits use the base
allowance; revisits do not grow the schedule. Counted segments also retain their
elapsed-time cap. The finite CLI exposes `slice-work`, `slice-max-work` and
`slice-growth`, and reports all three effective settings in portfolio statistics.

Resumable backtrack arms charge decisions, propagation visits and completed LP work to
their scheduler: one slice node per 4000 propagation visits and per 600 LP work units.
Construction is charged once; fractional charges carry forward and overspending is
repaid by later slices. The solve-wide decision/move cap keeps its separate currency.

Slices yield before consuming an alternative or after a completed node. A pending
sibling cannot be skipped when yielding, and reset discards a pending alternative.
Constructor refutations return immediately, and a rejected last root leaf can finish
exhausted even if its completed work consumes the slice. The conversion is not an
elapsed-time guarantee: propagation fixpoints, construction, expensive factor calls,
an LP solve and value-probe batches can overrun a slice. Local-search arms follow the
[retained handle contract](15.4-lifetime.md#handles-and-exclusive-ownership).

### Private CP preparation

General CP optimization handles retain one private construction cursor through
projection, state buffers, occurrence and event indexes, watch registration, root
queue seeding and propagation, assumptions, LP planning and shared-search
installation. Long construction scans checkpoint every 256 entries or visits,
including scans inside a wide factor's index lists. Array allocation, one domain
materialization, a factor invocation and a payload snapshot remain atomic. A handoff
that reaches cancellation or its segment deadline returns before entering search; the
next segment starts traversal. The packed native-SAT optimization path and public
satisfaction/repair constructors keep their eager lifecycle.

A paused cursor cannot publish an assignment or an incomplete fixpoint as a
refutation; a completed root conflict can. Successful installation transfers the
prepared state and work ledger once, so assumption pins, watcher entries and LP plans
are not restarted on resume, and root propagation keeps pending wakes between
batches. Construction spends wall time without manufacturing decisions or LP work.
Closing drops the cursor and any installed LP owner; installation exceptions close the
handle before propagating. See [finite propagation](14.2-propagation.md#finite-propagation).

### Polynomial root preparation

Polynomial complete workers defer component initialization into their retained
handle, and clausal optimization with additional components does the same instead of
opening its root eagerly. Root normalization, bounded witness probing and projection
retain completed phases and projection matrices across slices. Subresultant
preparation checkpoints matrix rows, Bareiss cells, exact-division reductions and
batches of 256 monomial products; one rational operation, polynomial snapshot or the
final cell check can overrun a slice. Every continuation keeps the same native
allowance. Unfinished initialization publishes neither a point nor a refutation.

### Auxiliary incumbent evaluation

An auxiliary arm retains its preparation and receives a bounded search trial after
an incumbent arrives; one admitted before the incumbent enters its trial when the
ordinary policy next selects it, at the base probe allowance. Trial continuations run before ordinary policy selection,
after every initial sibling has been admitted; explicit minimum shares and eligible
core proof continuations keep precedence. The trial uses the current ordinary
allowance, clipped to its remaining budget, and does not advance the shared allowance
ladder. Search after preparation is capped at twelve maximum work allowances and four
base time allowances; preparation has twelve base time allowances. Atomic overspend
consumes the trial rather than buying fresh segments, and debt repayment grants no
fresh work. Productive arms return to the ordinary policy; unproductive arms retire
without plateau reseeding. The budget belongs to the invocation and never renews
native arithmetic quotas.

## Preparation revisits

Before the first incumbent, an optimization arm with pending private preparation, or
a local-search arm that has not executed an instruction, receives preparation
revisits after all initial siblings have been admitted. Unfinished arms rotate in arm
order at the base allowance and regular family time share until they enter search or
terminate. Explicit minimum shares and the feasibility elapsed floor bound repeated
revisits; no revisit delays an unadmitted sibling. Dedicated lanes resume their own
handles directly.

Before the first accepted witness, family selection rewards observable progress
toward feasibility. A selected complete-search family runs its arm with the least
charged work; shared clauses, root fixings and objective floors stay credited but
earn the family no extra turns, because deduction throughput does not predict the
first witness. Observable violation reductions can earn a family more turns, and a
positive-work heuristic segment without progress supplies plateau feedback.
Private preparation, cancelled initialization and zero-work pauses supply no such
failure signal, and an internal violation score never installs an incumbent.

Complete workers also keep an elapsed coverage floor until the first witness or
refutation. Each live complete worker is owed an equal part of one neutral family
share of active segment time, with one base time allowance of aggregate lag;
heuristic bootstrap and local search share the other family. Below-floor complete
workers run before ordinary family selection, and coverage can interrupt preparation
revisits without restarting their cursors. Retired, deferred and auxiliary proof
workers owe no coverage. An accepted incumbent ends the floor. The lag and
cooperative overspend bound the guarantee; no fixed time or result is promised to a
worker, and because coverage depends on elapsed time, a single lane and seed do not
make the full schedule reproducible.

## Reseeding and arm failures

Optimization portfolios can reseed stale resumable arms after an incumbent;
`reseed-stale-threshold` defaults to 3, with 0 disabling it. Restart-configured
backtrack arms count equivalent base work segments, so a time-capped return
contributes its charged work divided by the base work, capped at one, and short
fragments cannot destroy retained traversal. Observable families and whole lanes
count each positive-work return as one segment; debt-only pauses do not count, and
accepted improvements clear accumulated credit. Backends defer reseeding while
bounded preparation owns pending work, and a preparation that earns an improvement
can continue across slices before the policy chooses again.

Complete-search arms without a restart schedule retain their traversal, because
closing a proof gap need not produce a better incumbent; imported cutoffs still
apply. Reseeding preserves terminal verdicts. Each arm keeps completed-handle totals
separately from its live snapshot and merges a closed handle once, including when
reseeding or stopping the pool.

Ordinary arm exceptions retire their producer and leave siblings running. The
schedule keeps one diagnostic per arm with its identity, operation, segment, charged
work, exception type, message and cause trace, capped at 128, 1024 and 4096
characters. Output callback exceptions still propagate, and source-refuted claims
remain quarantine faults. Unsupported local-search arms are filtered from mixed pools;
local-search-only requests keep their own behavior.

## Incumbent guidance

FlatZinc search hints select tiered variable and value heuristics without inheriting
the free-search preset's phase saving or restart schedule; the hint's
solution-guided value wrapper stays available without saved phases taking
precedence. Optimization updates guidance only after admitting a complete improving
incumbent. Solution-guided selectors keep their first descent, then adopt better
verified pooled incumbents when traversal resumes, without discarding their trail or
learned state. Other pooled solution phasing keeps its restart-boundary import policy.
