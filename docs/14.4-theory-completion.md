# 14.4 Theory completion

Finite search assigns Boolean and integer variables; arithmetic theories complete or
refute the remaining coordinates. This subchapter describes how those theory owners
take part in the shared traversal: the encoders they install, how several owners agree
on shared coordinates, and how completed values reach the published model. The
theories themselves are the subject of [Part IV](18-theories.md).

## Theory completion

A component's partial consistency check or relaxation witness does not accept a
complete source assignment: every participating owner must finish its required check
before publication. [Theory contracts](18-theories.md) define the fragment-specific
checks and evidence. Difference graphs, exact linear systems and polynomial completion
keep their own state while sharing the traversal's source facts and decision lifetime.
Integer integrality and finite factors cannot be dropped to obtain an arithmetic
witness.

Exact linear components wait for finite-owned coordinates before proposing arithmetic
splits or accepting their final completion. Finite publications supply scoped bounds
and exact fixed values; the finite owner retains hole membership. An isolated
arithmetic service with a point outside a source value set withholds that witness
instead of treating its bound hull as the source. Retraction invalidates both peers'
scoped outcomes. A resolved comparison remains asserted through its LP scope, and
deeper checks reuse it until retraction removes that scope.

Difference components can imply comparison guards from checked active source paths.
These facts carry clause-form reasons and enter the ordinary shared Boolean fixpoint,
unlike candidate preferences. Transient bounds are excluded unless their complete
source premises are represented. See [difference proofs](19-difference.md).

Finite satisfaction over a native real absolute value uses complete linear completion
at each fixed discrete assignment. A private exact component explores its arithmetic
alternatives under the containing execution controls, then returns source real
coordinates, a refuted discrete assignment or an undecided completion. Its private
decisions do not change source integer ownership.

Open quadratic integer optimization supplies exact complementary wide predicates
through a theory brancher. Its node policy prunes against certified real bounds and
checked feasible incumbents, without turning objective cutoffs into source
infeasibility conflicts. See
[nonlinear integer quadratic objectives](23-nonlinear-integer.md#quadratic-objectives).

## Selected theory encoders

Prepared factor encoders supply the full relations used by shared arithmetic
components; admission and component construction consume the same selected capture.
Variable ownership is independent of factor participation: finite declarations retain
finite state and choices, while a propagator and encoder can consume one factor
together. Component descriptors distinguish primary `factorIndices` from overlapping
`participatingFactorIndices`, retaining one authoritative coordinate owner.

Linear relations retain conjunction/disjunction structure, row activators, Boolean
terms, strictness and original factor attribution. Private real coordinates stay in
the combined exact model; their disjunction and disequality decisions use
component-owned atoms with the same decision-level retraction as source relations,
and exact witness publication strips them before typed source contribution. Integer
difference admission checks complete selected relations and the safety of their
combined graph. Polynomial installation conjoins factor relations and all private
auxiliary definitions before completion checks the combined system.

Finite integer embeddings in polynomial queries are private reals whose fixings come
from finite state; an unfixed interval remains a relaxation and supplies no witness
authority. Relaxation selection cannot omit an encoder's enforcing relation or grant
completeness, and the original source checker remains required when numerical
execution proposes a point.

A composed finite registration omits duplicate original point checks for factors whose
mandatory arithmetic peer installs a complete enforcing encoding, such as a required
polynomial peer that identifies the finite factors it enforces. The finite adapter
still rejects interrupted fixpoints, and every arithmetic check must succeed before the
shared leaf is accepted. Standalone finite projections retain their original check
obligations.

## Arithmetic coordinate agreement

Prepared registration collections with several arithmetic completion groups install
`SourceCoordinateAgreementComponent` after their ordinary participants. Each group
must implement `SourceCoordinateAgreement`; unsupported groups leave the combined
result indeterminate. Standard preparation retains one combined arithmetic owner and
installs no agreement participant.

The exact-linear adapter exchanges normalized source bounds through the neutral atom
registry. Peers enforce the same predicates, including strictness, guarded rows,
arbitrary-precision integers and active branch conditions. Published facts stay at
their shared decision level; retraction removes conditions whose premises disappear.

Each source integer/real coordinate has one declared registration owner, and explicit
peer collections guard numeric contributions against those declarations. After
fragment checks, agreement reads the existing immutable typed snapshot and validates
it against each peer's current source rows and conditions. It neither decodes another
witness nor combines private fragment points. A rejected chosen point alone does not
refute all other source completions.

Rational linear agreement admits exact integers and rational real coordinates.
Missing, oversized, algebraic/symbolic or otherwise unsupported contributions remain
indeterminate rather than rounded. Agreement supplies neither general theory
combination, procedure completeness nor optimization/bound authority. It uses the
existing invocation cancellation, complete-check accounting and caller native work
meter, and opens no fresh solver or allowance.

## Contributed model ownership

Components contribute immutable values through typed keys, with one owner per key.
The assembled model captures its map and each typed key's ownership hook at
publication; reads retain the associated value type without copying. Exact source
integer and real keys preserve arbitrary-precision and rational coordinates. Extensions
retain open keys for arrays, functions and native theory values; their captured
payloads must be immutable. CP-learned Boolean clauses remain natively owned by CP
while their shared consequences reach theory peers. See
[API migration and mappings](30-api.md#typed-contributions-and-migration).
