# 15.3 Scoring and neighborhoods

The scoring axis of [Strategies and moves](15.1-strategies.md) values a candidate
by its effect on the model. With the definitions of
[Preparation and definitions](15.2-preparation.md) in place, that effect reaches
beyond the coordinates a move writes: every derived output and every factor reading
one can change. This subchapter describes how a candidate is graded exactly and
reversibly, how factors select their scoring projection, how continuous arithmetic
is scored, and the structured neighborhoods that NValue, Cumulative and Diffn
contribute.

## Scoring and probe restoration

A primitive move without definition readers is scored directly from its
invariants' `deltaIf*` results. Compounds without definition readers sum their
parts' linear objective contributions for distinct coordinates; repeated-coordinate
linear deltas use a probe of the final values.

Moves that feed definitions score the complete propagated assignment through a
reversible **probe**. A probe applies all compound inputs together, runs one
definition pass over the affected cone, reads hard cost and objective, then
restores the saved inputs and outputs directly. Feasible grading reads hard cost and
a built-in linear or functional objective from the same probe, and a hard violation
rejects the move before its objective is valued. Probe activity and best-cost
observations are suppressed, and real moves inside probes do not advance the
committed row-refresh cadence. Weighted probes rescan only factors whose degrees
change, in factor-id order, keeping the floating-point accumulation order of a full
scan.

`ArgminJump` builds coordinated integer targets and maintained-Boolean inverse
repairs through the generic move sink before comparing their complete weighted
deltas. Each sampled coordinate contributes at most one strict improvement over
staying put. Small domains are enumerated; wider ones keep a sampled-target cap.

### Linear repairs

Linear repairs offer nearest-clamped targets in term order, keeping partial interior
and endpoint repairs. Inequality repairs add distinct targets rounded through domain
holes toward the requested truth, using the coefficient sign for direction; wide rows
use one bounded term sample for both target sets. Reified repairs offer valid
candidates in indicator and term order, then rounded alternatives, and body targets
must match the retained or flipped indicator.

### Comparison clauses

Comparison-clause scores substitute a target into every matching literal and keep
the minimum saturated shortfall. Violated clauses offer admissible literal bounds in
addition to neighbouring values, rounding inequalities toward the satisfying side of
domain holes and declining unreachable equalities. When zero satisfies a violated
literal on a maintained conditional product, the clause can add at most two
coordinated product repairs through binary equality channels.

## Selected scoring projections

`ScoringProjectionProvider` selects a factor's invariant factory, scoring
participation and assignment-check authority from immutable factor data. The
factory receives the worker's move domains; built-in linear rows keep exact sums
when those domains can exceed the machine-integer range, and use compact signed-word
scoring when activity and deltas fit. A custom factory must preserve the source
relation at zero violation and supply consistent deltas for its advertised role.

An explicit inert score contributes no occurrences and needs an explicit reason; it
cannot claim an exact check, so an enforcing peer or separate source certification
must cover the factor. Unsupported scoring refuses construction, and returning
`NoInvariant` for a declared active role is rejected. Open heuristic models keep
their prepared scoring capture separately from the neutral source, and scheduling
windows and dense-timeline restrictions apply to built-in scorers only.

## Continuous arithmetic scores

`ContinuousProduct`, real `Power`, `RealProduct` and `RealAbs` grade floating
assignments with native invariants while retaining their exact source relations.
Scores substitute every aliased role together, repairs respect declared real bounds
and keep both inverse signs, and nonfinite arithmetic never scores zero.
Finite-choice products also grade operand membership and guard truth and propose
the nearest declared operand. These scores are heuristic: zero violation requires
exact completion of the whole source relation in
[Completion, restarts and ownership](15.4-lifetime.md#continuous-candidate-completion).

`GridProduct` and `GridPower` instead grade their declared rounded index relation
with integer deltas, respecting aliases, domain holes and channel ownership. Exact
grid membership supplies no continuous-source witness; approximation metadata and
whole-source validation remain required.

## NValue neighborhoods

A satisfied NValue factor offers count-preserving coordinate moves and bounded
**value-class elimination**: remove one distinct value by moving every coordinate
holding it to surviving values its root domain admits, and reconcile the count for
the factor's mode. Frozen, maintained or foreign-owned coordinates and moves that
would change presence literals decline the whole class. Count-decreasing repairs use
the same operator when NValue is the only violation, including an objective bound
pursued as a constraint. Each call makes at most 24 attempts and offers at most four
eliminations of at most 32 coordinates each.

An elimination usually breaks surrounding constraints, so up to four sampled
eliminations are reconciled on a scratch assignment. Bounded reconciliation repairs
clauses, XOR parity, Element cells and unary channels, protecting the counted
coordinates and the count, and the best candidate may follow the directed repair
chain for at most eight steps. Only a complete zero-violation proposal is offered,
and NValue satisfaction alone establishes neither feasibility nor improvement.

## Cumulative neighborhoods

An integer move updates every task reading the coordinate as a start, duration or
demand, including aliases and coordinates shared with capacity, and deltas use the
final combined load rather than summing independent scores. Overload start swaps
filter on the joint profile change of both coordinates, applying and restoring only
the affected footprints; at most four are offered, around the peak task and its
partners.

Overload repair can also remove a maintained product duration through a binary
equality channel and its choice predicate, drawing at most 16 such moves per call,
and can swap the choices of conditional durations between task pairs. Per-worker
cursors advance the starting peak task and the task pairs, so early candidates cannot
consume every call's budget. Start-time repairs skip absent tasks and tasks with zero
duration or demand. Conditional-duration proposals precede start proposals and swaps.

During infeasibility, weighted and raw ranking interpolates Cumulative overloads
within their compressed grade buckets: each factor adds a fraction below one grade
unit, so moves that shrink an overload rank ahead of moves that do not, even at
equal integer cost. Committed grades use the same saturated payload, and weight and
stall schedules treat a lower fraction at equal cost as progress.

## Diffn neighborhoods

A satisfied Diffn layout offers up to four adjacency, boundary-compaction and paired
reorder or translation moves, alongside equal-footprint swaps. Relocations sample at
most eight rectangles and check at most 24 admissible targets. Geometry reads every
aliased position and variable size at its final target, compares endpoints beyond
the Long range exactly, matches the invariant's strict or nonstrict zero-size
semantics, and declines moves that affect more than eight rectangles.

An admitted relocation can reconcile up to two decreasing scalar envelopes outside
its Diffn scope, such as a makespan bound fed by rectangle ends. Integral linear
inequalities with a unit negative output coefficient supply exact lower targets
after the geometry writes, within shared per-call visit limits. These are private
candidate values, not deductions. Geometry checks establish only the proposing
Diffn's satisfaction; complete scoring and publication checks cover the rest.

During infeasibility, root repairs can also keep satisfied variable-array Element
results intact by coordinating admissible destination cells, probing at most four
variants over at most 32 selected factors; the original candidates remain available.
